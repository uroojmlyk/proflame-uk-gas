Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you get an engineer to me?

For emergencies we offer a 1 hour, sameday and 24 hour callout at £130 inc VAT. Engineers can be with you in as little as 1 hour. We also offer a next day 8am-8pm service 7 days a week at £110 inc VAT.

What areas do you cover?

We cover the BA, BS, GL and SN postcode areas across the South West. We're based in Filton by the M5/M4 interchange, which lets us reach most of the region quickly.

Are your engineers qualified and registered?

Yes. Our engineers are Gas Safe registered and OFTEC registered, Worcester Bosch trained and approved, Vaillant accredited installers, LPG qualified, CRB checked and ex British Gas, with 25 years of plumbing experience.

Do you offer a warranty on your work?

Yes. We provide a 1 year warranty on all works completed. We're not satisfied until you are, and we'll resolve any issues promptly and effectively.

Can I get a fixed price before work starts?

Absolutely. We're happy to attend your property to carry out a home survey and offer you a fixed price quote before any work begins.

Do you work with landlords and letting agents?

Yes. We have social housing experience and work as a letting and estate agent contractor, including safety checks and certification.
