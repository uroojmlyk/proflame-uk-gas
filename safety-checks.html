Landlord gas safety checks (CP12)

By law, landlords must have a gas safety check carried out every 12 months on all gas appliances and flues in their rental properties. This includes boilers, gas fires, hobs, ovens and any other gas-powered equipment. A Gas Safe registered engineer must carry out the inspection, and you must provide a copy of the certificate to your tenants within 28 days of the check.

We provide CP12 certificates for landlords, letting agents and social housing providers across the South West. Our checks are thorough, and we'll highlight any issues that need attention so you can address them quickly.

What's included in a gas safety check

  • Visual inspection of all gas appliances and flues
  • Gas leak testing on the entire installation
  • Checking correct gas pressure and burner operation
  • Verifying ventilation requirements are met
  • Checking all safety devices are functioning
  • Issuing a Gas Safety Certificate (CP12) on completion

Homeowner safety checks

Even if you're not a landlord, regular gas safety checks are a sensible way to keep your family safe and your appliances running efficiently. We offer safety checks for homeowners too, covering all gas appliances in your property. This is particularly important if you have older appliances or are buying a new home.

Gas appliance installations

We also install gas appliances safely, including cookers, hobs, gas fires and boilers. All installations are carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers and comply with current Building Regulations.

Common Questions

Safety Check FAQs

The questions we're asked most often about gas safety.

How often should a landlord gas safety check be carried out?

Legally, landlords must have a gas safety check carried out every 12 months by a Gas Safe registered engineer. The check must be completed before any new tenant moves in.

What does a gas safety check include?

A gas safety check involves inspecting all gas appliances and flues to ensure they are working safely, checking for leaks, verifying correct gas pressure and burner operation, and ensuring ventilation requirements are met. A Gas Safety Certificate (CP12) is issued upon completion.

How much does a landlord gas safety certificate cost?

A landlord gas safety certificate costs £75 plus VAT, plus any additional travel and parking charges. If any repairs are needed, these are quoted separately.

What appliances are covered by a gas safety check?

All gas appliances in the property, including boilers, gas fires, hobs, ovens, and any other gas-powered equipment. The check covers every appliance accessible to the engineer.
