Boiler replacements and new installations

If your boiler is beyond repair, or you're upgrading to a more efficient model, we can help. We install quality appliances from Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Glow-worm and other leading manufacturers. What's included in a boiler installation

  • Free home survey and fixed price quote
  • Supply and installation of your new boiler
  • Removal and disposal of the old boiler
  • Flue and condensate pipework as required
  • System flush and water treatment (if required)
  • Electrical controls and thermostat installation
  • Building regulations self-certification (Gas Safe notification)
  • Manufacturer warranty registration

Combi, system or heat-only?

Not sure which boiler is right for your home? We'll talk you through the options:

  • Combi boilers heat water on demand and don't need a hot water cylinder - ideal for smaller homes with one or two bathrooms.
  • System boilers work with a hot water cylinder and are great for homes with multiple bathrooms or high demand for hot water.
  • Heat-only boilers (regular/conventional) are the traditional setup, also with a cylinder, ideal for homes with older radiator systems.

Gas vs oil vs LPG

We install all types of fuel boilers - natural gas, LPG and oil. If you're off the mains gas grid, we can advise on the best heating solution for your property, including the option to move away from oil to a low-carbon system like an air source heat pump.

Common Questions

Boiler Installation FAQs

The questions we're asked most often about boiler installs.

How much does a new boiler cost?

The cost depends on the model, the size of your home and any additional work needed such as flue extension or pipework changes. We'll attend your property, survey the job and give you a fixed price quote before any work begins.

How long does a boiler installation take?

A straightforward like-for-like swap usually takes one day. More complex installations, such as moving the boiler to a new location, can take two to three days.

What warranty do I get on a new boiler?

New boilers come with a manufacturer's warranty which can range from 2 to 10+ years depending on the brand and model. Worcester Bosch and Vaillant boilers we install can carry extended warranties when serviced annually by a registered engineer.

Should I repair or replace my boiler?

If your boiler is over 10 years old and frequently breaks down, a replacement is usually the better long-term option. We'll give you an honest assessment of whether your boiler is worth repairing before recommending a replacement.
