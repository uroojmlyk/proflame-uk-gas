Why annual boiler servicing matters

A well-maintained boiler is safer, more efficient and less likely to break down. Annual servicing is the single most important thing you can do to keep your heating system running reliably, and it's required to keep your manufacturer's warranty valid. Most boiler warranties are void the moment a boiler misses its yearly service, so skipping a service to save a few pounds is false economy.

What a boiler service includes

A standard service typically takes around 30 minutes to 1 hour and includes:

  • Full visual inspection of the boiler, flue and controls
  • Checking and cleaning the burner and ignition
  • Inspection of the heat exchanger and combustion chamber
  • Flue gas analysis and efficiency readings
  • Pressure test and system water pressure check
  • Testing all safety devices and controls
  • Written service report with efficiency record

Full strip-down service

For many boilers, a standard service is not enough to meet the manufacturer's servicing instructions. A full strip-down service involves disassembling the boiler to access and clean the heat exchanger, burner and other internal components. This costs £150 plus VAT and is required for many modern boilers to stay within warranty.

We'll advise you on the right level of service for your boiler. If you're unsure, we'll inspect your boiler and recommend the most appropriate service.

Boiler repair and fault finding

If your boiler is showing an error code, making strange noises, or failing to produce hot water, our engineers can diagnose and repair the problem. We carry common parts on the van, which is a big part of why over 98% of our jobs are fixed on the first visit.

Common Questions

Boiler Servicing FAQs

The questions we're asked most often about boiler servicing.

How often should I service my boiler?

Once a year. An annual service keeps your boiler running safely and efficiently, catches small faults before they become breakdowns, and keeps your manufacturer's warranty valid.

How long does a boiler service take?

A standard service takes approximately 30 minutes to 1 hour. A full strip-down service takes around 2 hours.

What is a strip-down service?

A strip-down service involves disassembling the boiler to access and clean the heat exchanger, burner and other internal components. This is required for many boilers to comply with the manufacturer's instructions and costs £150 plus VAT.

Will a service void my warranty?

No - it's the opposite. A service carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer is required to keep your warranty valid. Missing a service is what voids the warranty.
